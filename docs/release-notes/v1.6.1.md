### Fixed (flame sensor)
- After a restart the cloud flame entity is often still loading. The relay was then counted as burn for a few seconds, creating a fake short burn. Now an unavailable flame counts as no burn for the first 10 minutes; only a longer outage falls back to the relay.
- *Flame share of demand* and *Ignitions per demand hour* only count flames inside the heat-demand periods. Before, burns recorded with the relay alone (before the flame sensor was set) made the share read 100 %.
