### Card: easier to read
- **Tabs**: *Now*, *History*, *Curve*, *Comfort*. On a phone you see one screen at a time; the last tab is remembered. `tabs: false` keeps everything stacked
- **Health summary** at the top of *Now*: condensing, ignitions per hour, pressure and comfort verdict, coloured green / amber / red
- **New 24 h chart**: flow and return temperatures from the recorder, the flow target while heating, the flames as a lane and the heat demand as a band. Hover or tap anywhere to read the values at that minute
- **The 24 h chart is zoomable** on time, down to 15 minutes: drag a range, pinch, double-tap, Ctrl + wheel or the + / − / ⟲ buttons
- Live values in a tidy 3 × 2 grid; tiles grouped as *Burns · 24 h* and *Today*; pressure moved to the health summary
- New option in the visual editor: *tabs*

### Upgrade
Update via HACS, restart Home Assistant and press Ctrl+F5 (or clear the companion app cache) to load the new card.
