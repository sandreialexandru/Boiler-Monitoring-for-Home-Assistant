### New: Comfort vs outdoor, with a tuning verdict
Pick your room thermostat (⋮ → **Reconfigure** → *Room thermostat*) and Boiler Monitor compares the rooms with the thermostat target, hour by hour, against the outdoor temperature:
- `sensor.<name>_comfort_verdict`: **Curve OK**, **Slope too low / too high**, **Offset too low / too high**, or **Not enough data yet**, with a confidence level and the numbers behind it
- `sensor.<name>_comfort_gap_24h`: rooms − target over the last 24 steady hours
- Card: verdict box with plain advice (adapted to weather compensation or fixed flow), plus a zoomable chart with a comfort band
- Night set-back hours and the recovery hour after raising the setting are ignored, so they don't look like "too cold"
- Designed for on/off thermostats: a curve that is too high is detected from short cycles and overshoot, a curve that is too low from rooms that can't reach the setting while the burner runs almost non-stop

### New: Warm-up time and pre-heat
- Learns how many **minutes per °C** the house needs to warm up, at each outdoor temperature (measured after every pause of ≥ 1 h)
- `sensor.<name>_heat_up_time_per_degc` and `sensor.<name>_pre_heat_time` (to the thermostat target, from now)
- New action **`boiler_monitor.estimate_preheat`** (with response): when to start heating to reach X °C at a given time. It uses the hourly **weather forecast** if you pick a weather entity. Ready-made morning pre-heat automation in the README and `examples/automations.yaml`
- Card: "Warming up the house" chart and summary

### Upgrade
Update via HACS, restart Home Assistant, then **Reconfigure** to pick the thermostat (and optionally a weather entity). Ctrl+F5 in the browser to load the new card. The comfort verdict needs about a day of heating to appear; warm-ups appear after the first long pause.
