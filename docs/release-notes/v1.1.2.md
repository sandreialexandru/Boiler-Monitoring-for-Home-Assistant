### New: zoomable charts
The **Heating curve / Fixed flow** and **Burn vs outdoor** charts can now be zoomed:
- **Mouse:** drag a box to zoom into it, double-click to zoom in on a spot, Ctrl/⌘ + wheel to zoom
- **Touch:** pinch to zoom, double-tap to zoom in, drag with one finger to move around when zoomed
- **Buttons** in the top-right corner of each chart: **+**, **−**, **⟲** (reset)

### Better tooltips for overlapping points
Hovering or tapping now lists **all** points under the pointer (up to three in full, "+N more" for the rest), and a ring marks the closest one. Zoom in to separate them.

The zoom level is kept while the card refreshes with new data.

### Upgrade
Update via HACS, restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+F5).
