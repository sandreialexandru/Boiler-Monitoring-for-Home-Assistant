### Added — optional data for more boiler types
All of these are optional. Existing setups keep working unchanged; add them via ⋮ → **Reconfigure** if your boiler integration (OpenTherm, ems-esp, ebusd, Ariston, Vaillant…) exposes them.

- **Hot water active**: burner time during domestic hot water is not counted as heating; new status `hot_water` (blue on the card)
- **Modulation** + **max / min power (kW)**: heat power, heat energy today (kWh), energy per degree-day, average modulation and time at minimum. With these, the balance point is fitted on kWh instead of burn hours
- **Burner-starts counter** from the boiler: starts today and per demand hour; the *Ignitions* pill uses it
- **Gas meter**: gas today and per degree-day, in the meter's unit; meter resets are ignored
- **Numeric burner threshold**: `>60` / `<5` as the "burner on" state, e.g. a smart plug's power in W
- **Thermoregulation from a select or sensor**, with a configurable "on" state (e.g. `auto`)

### Card
- *Energy* and *Gas* tile groups, kWh / gas / boiler starts in the day tooltips, *Energy vs outdoor* chart when power is known

### Upgrade
Update via HACS and restart Home Assistant. Stored statistics are kept. Ctrl+F5 in the browser.
