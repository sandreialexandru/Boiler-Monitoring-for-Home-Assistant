### New: flame sensor (relay = heat demand, flame = burn)
- New optional field **Flame sensor** (⋮ → Reconfigure), e.g. `binary_sensor.ariston_is_flame_on`. With it, the burner entity is treated as heat demand and a burn is counted only while there is demand **and** flame.
- Flame without demand (domestic hot water) is ignored. Pauses the boiler makes on its own while the relay still asks for heat are no longer counted as burn time, and each ignition is its own cycle, so internal short-cycling finally shows up.
- Ignitions the cloud flame sensor misses are detected from a ≥ 4 °C jump in the flow temperature during demand and counted as 1-minute burns (shown lighter on the timeline).
- Warm-up time and the heating-effect check follow the demand, not the individual flames.
- New status `demand` (heat demand, flame off) and three new sensors: heat demand time today, flame share of demand (24 h), ignitions per demand hour (24 h).
- Card: pale demand band behind the flames on the 24 h timeline, three new tiles, demand time in the day tooltips.

### Upgrade
Update via HACS and restart Home Assistant, then ⋮ → **Reconfigure** and pick the flame sensor. Statistics recorded before stay as they were (relay time); run the `reset_statistics` action if you want a clean start. Ctrl+F5 in the browser to load the new card version.
