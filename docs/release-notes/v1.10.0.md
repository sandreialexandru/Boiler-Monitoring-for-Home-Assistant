### Added
- **Heat loss (W/°C)**: how much heat the home loses per °C of indoor–outdoor difference, over the last 7 full days, with a 30-day fit that also estimates the free heat (people, appliances, sun). It does not depend on the weather, so it shows directly whether balancing, insulation or the neighbours heating made a difference. Needs indoor and outdoor temperatures and the heat in kWh: modulation + max power, or a gas meter (kWh or m³). Ariston NET users can use *central heating total energy consumption* as the gas meter
- **Recommended curve**: the flow temperatures that would keep the rooms exactly at the thermostat setting with the burner running most of the time. New sensors *Recommended flow at 0 °C* and *Recommended curve slope*, and on the card a dotted line on the curve chart plus the advice in numbers: flow at 0 °C and −10 °C now → recommended, and the slope factor. Needs flow temperature, thermostat and outdoor temperature

### Card
- *Recommended curve* on the Curve tab (on the Comfort tab when there is no flow setpoint sensor)
- *Heat loss* on the History tab

### Upgrade
Update via HACS and restart Home Assistant. Both work on the statistics already stored. Ctrl+F5 in the browser.
