### Fixed: absurd balance point
- With a nearly flat or noisy burn-vs-outdoor line (mild days, few days of data, neighbours' heating still off) the line crossed zero at meaningless values such as 258 °C
- The balance point is now reported only with at least 4 complete days (3 of them with real burn), a falling line with R² ≥ 0.3 and a result between 0 and 25 °C. Otherwise the sensor is `unknown` and the card shows *Balance point: not reliable yet*
- New attribute `regression.balance_reason`: `few_days`, `weak_fit` or `out_of_range`

### Upgrade
Update via HACS and restart Home Assistant. Ctrl+F5 in the browser.
