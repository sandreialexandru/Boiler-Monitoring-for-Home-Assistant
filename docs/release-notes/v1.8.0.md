### Fixed
- **Double-counted ignition**: when the flow jump came before the (late) flame entity, the burn was counted twice and the second one as a short cycle with a false alert. The two are now merged into one burn
- **Typical pause showed the night** (e.g. 714 min while the boiler cycled every 6 min). Burn and pause are now medians and, with a flame sensor, only pauses inside a heat demand count
- **Units**: temperatures in °F / K and pressure in mbar / kPa / Pa / psi are converted to °C and bar, so thresholds and statistics are right for every setup. The 24 h chart converts the history too

### Changed
- Statistics are saved every 10 minutes and on shutdown instead of every minute, in a smaller file. Much less wear on SD cards
- Card labels: *Typical burn*, *Typical pause*

### Upgrade
Update via HACS and restart Home Assistant. Stored statistics are kept. Ctrl+F5 in the browser.
